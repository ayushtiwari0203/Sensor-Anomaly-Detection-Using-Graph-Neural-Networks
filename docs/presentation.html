1. The Problem: Non-Euclidean Industrial Telemetry

Industrial facilities rely on hundreds of interconnected sensors (vibration, heat, hydraulic pressure). Traditional ML models fail because they ignore topological sensor couplings.

  • Structural Blindness: Standard models ignore cross-sensor spatial correlations.
  • Early Degradation Signals: Micro-fractures and bearing degradation manifest as subtle correlation shifts long before catastrophic downtime.
  • Non-Euclidean Representation: Graph Neural Networks embed telemetry into structural manifolds.
